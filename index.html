Problem Concurrent stock claims caused double-assignments; proxy blocks and fake warranty screenshots caused stock loss.
Architecture Lease-locked worker queue, SQLite WAL + Supabase RPC, rotating proxy circuit breaker & AI Vision OCR matching.
Outcome Zero double-claims under load, >95% card OCR verification accuracy, automated Telegram ops alerts.
Python Flask Blueprints SQLite WAL Supabase RPC AI Vision OCR Rotating Proxies
Verified Engineering Mechanics:
  • Atomic Lease-Locking Queue: Workers claim jobs with 180s expiring lease tokens and fingerprint guards so stale workers never overwrite fresh state.
